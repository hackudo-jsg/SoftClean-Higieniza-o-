# SoftClean Higienização — design brief

- **Design read:** A premium, approachable local service brand for families and drivers in western Mato Grosso who want clean, cared-for upholstery and an easy way to request a quote.
- **Concept spine:** The clean reveal — each scroll reveals another layer of care, from fabric texture to the full, refreshed living space.
- **Delivery tier:** cinema, with restrained editorial composition and an image-led hero.
- **Locked palette:** forest green `#176B45`, soft white `#FFFFFF`, pale green `#F2F7F3`, ink `#17251D`. The green ties the site to the existing SoftClean identity and is used as a single accent rather than a glow.
- **Locked type:** Cabinet Grotesk-like expressive sans for display + Inter Tight-like neutral sans for body; fallback system sans stacks.
- **Animation mode:** animated-website — user chose premium cinematic animations and animated scrolling.
- **Journey shape:** single-shot concept expressed as a scroll-driven image journey because the video-generation service had no available credits; no fabricated video is shipped. The hero image scales and shifts with scroll, and semantic chapters reveal in sequence.
- **Journey:** 1) the sofa and clean home establish trust; 2) close-up fabric detail introduces the care; 3) upholstery and automotive service chapters explain the offer; 4) local coverage chapters make the three municipalities explicit; 5) final contact section moves visitors to WhatsApp.
- **World grammar:** photoreal upholstery, natural side light, pale neutral home, muted forest-green accents, realistic fabric and extraction tools, center-safe framing.
- **Mobile framing:** keep the sofa and service message central, stack service blocks, provide sticky WhatsApp access, reduce animation under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Delivery budget:** one optimized hero still; CSS/JS motion avoids heavy video downloads and remains responsive on mobile.
- **Section plan:** image-led hero; asymmetric service overview; split home/automotive service panel; clean numbered process; local service area; FAQ; high-contrast contact close.
- **Asset plan:** generated hero photo, typography-based wordmark until the original logo file is available, inline SVG favicon, generated image reused as Open Graph image.
- **CTA inventory:** primary hero WhatsApp button; underlined service-area links; outlined secondary CTA; final full-width WhatsApp action; floating WhatsApp button.
- **Second-read moment:** one oversized numeral “03” frames the three-city service area.
